# BUILD CARD — A03 T-House / Forest Pavilion v0.1

## Identity

- ID: A03
- Existing Station: Teahouse Forest Pavilion / 林中茶屋
- Role: high-fidelity slow spatial experience
- Class: H48 / Standard
- Status: Existing line entering spatial rebuild
- Priority: P2 after A00 and Balloon

This card keeps the historical Seleiumeaves / VRChat lineage but defines a more precise 2026 product target.

## Main test question

Can one small high-fidelity room prove VIRTURA’s spatial quality better than a large quantity of average environments?

## Program

- Forest Threshold
- Covered Approach
- Tea Room
- Water / Courtyard Edge
- Quiet Listening Seat
- Small Screen / media surface
- Garden View
- Exit / Reflection Route

## Spatial principle

T-House is intentionally smaller and more detailed than most Stations.

The project should prioritize:
- proportion;
- light;
- material;
- sound;
- view framing;
- slow movement;
- sitting distance;
- threshold behavior.

It should not become a generalized wellness app full of menus.

## Material family

- timber / dark wood used with restraint;
- stone;
- plaster/mineral surface;
- glass;
- water;
- scanned vegetation;
- selected Gaussian natural fragments.

Conventional mesh remains the navigation and collision base.

## Lighting

A dedicated authored lighting study is mandatory.

Required conditions:
- morning;
- overcast / mist;
- dusk / warm interior.

Do not solve atmosphere with bloom and fog alone.

## Sound

- localized water;
- forest bed;
- room tone;
- optional tea / object detail;
- no constant music requirement.

## Interaction

v0.1:
- walk / orbit;
- sit / focus viewpoints;
- one screen or spatial media element;
- lighting condition switch;
- optional Gaussian garden layer.

XR:
- comfort-first;
- real standing/sitting scale;
- no artificial head bob;
- no mandatory continuous joystick locomotion.

## Quality gate

- room remains convincing in still screenshots;
- material scale survives close inspection;
- lighting has a clear source logic;
- garden can degrade gracefully if Gaussian is unavailable;
- screen media and spatial media can coexist;
- the experience still works when no UI panel is visible.
